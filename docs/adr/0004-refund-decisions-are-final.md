# Refund Request decisions are final, and the first one counts

Once a Platform Admin approves or declines a Refund Request, the decision can't be changed. If two Platform Admins act on the same Open Refund Request, the first decision counts and the second is told it's already decided. A Purchaser whose request was declined can raise a new Refund Request. An approval can't be undone, because it has already revoked an Enrollment or voided Seats and committed Cadence to a Refund that later work will pay out. Allowing a reversal would mean un-revoking access and cancelling a Refund that may already be on its way.

## Consequences

- No "undo" on the review screen. An approval made by mistake is handled outside this feature (for example, by giving the person a new Enrollment).
- Deciding has to check that the request is still Open at the moment of the decision, not just when the screen was loaded.
